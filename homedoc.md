# MASTER PROMPT: PROFESSIONAL E-LEARNING PLATFORM HOMEPAGE

## ROLE

Act as a **Senior Product Designer, UX/UI Designer, Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO) Specialist, Brand Strategist, and Frontend Design Architect** with 10+ years of experience designing premium SaaS, EdTech, LMS, and digital learning platforms.

Your task is to design a **modern, premium, highly professional, marketing-focused homepage for an e-learning platform**.

The website must feel comparable in quality to leading modern EdTech and SaaS products.

Do NOT create a generic educational website.

The design should communicate:

* Trust
* Intelligence
* Career growth
* Professional development
* Innovation
* Accessibility
* Premium quality
* Measurable learning outcomes
* Modern technology
* Human-centered learning

The homepage must be designed to **convert visitors into learners**.

---

# 1. PRIMARY OBJECTIVE

Design an e-learning platform homepage that guides a visitor through this psychological journey:

**Attention → Understanding → Trust → Value → Social Proof → Desire → Action**

The visitor should quickly understand:

1. What the platform does
2. Who it is for
3. What they can learn
4. Why the platform is different
5. Why they should trust it
6. What results they can achieve
7. How they can get started

The primary conversion goal is:

**Start Learning**

Secondary goals:

* Explore Courses
* Explore Learning Paths
* View Programs
* Become an Instructor
* Contact Sales / Business Learning

# 40. IMPORTANT DESIGN RULES

Follow these rules strictly:

1. Do not overcrowd the page.
2. Do not use more than two font families.
3. Do not use excessive gradients.
4. Do not use fake statistics.
5. Do not invent partnerships.
6. Do not make unsupported claims.
7. Do not use too many CTA styles.
8. Do not make every element a card.
9. Do not use excessive shadows.
10. Do not sacrifice readability for aesthetics.
11. Do not use decorative elements that interfere with the learning experience.
12. Maintain strong visual hierarchy.
13. Use whitespace intentionally.
14. Prioritize conversion without making the website feel aggressive.
15. Make the platform feel trustworthy enough for someone to invest time and money in learning.
