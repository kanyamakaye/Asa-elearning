### Student Group and Course Access Management

The e-learning platform shall provide the ability to create and manage **student groups** and assign one or more courses to each group.

Each group shall consist of a defined set of students and may be associated with one or multiple courses. **Only students who are members of a specific group shall be authorized to access the courses assigned to that group.**

Students who are **not members of the group shall not be permitted to access, view, or participate in any course assigned exclusively to that group**.

The system shall therefore enforce group-based access control to ensure that course content is accessible only to authorized students based on their group membership.

**Key requirements:**

* Administrators shall be able to create and manage student groups.
* Administrators shall be able to add or remove students from groups.
* A group may be assigned one or multiple courses.
* Students shall only have access to courses assigned to groups to which they belong.
* Students who are not members of a group shall be denied access to that group's assigned courses.
* Removing a student from a group shall revoke their access to courses associated with that group, subject to any applicable access or enrollment rules.
